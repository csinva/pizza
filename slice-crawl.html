Arthur Avenue Slice Crawl

Five slice counters in Bronx Little Italy, walked east to west along 187th, then north up Arthur Ave to 191st. One slice each per stop, and pace yourself.

    House rules

    • Split slices if you're a group of four or more. Five full slices is a lot of pizza.
    • Ivana's is cash only (ATM inside), and it closes at 6 PM.
    • Pugsley has the most seating, so save sitting down for the last stop.
    • Want dessert after? Arthur Ave is lined with pastry shops for a cannoli on the way out.

    Hours are Saturday hours from Google listings as of Oct 14, 2026. Small shops change things, so check the listing before you head out.
